Most Important Rules

  • Always practice safety
  • Be a good listener
  • Respect the classroom space
  • Respect the equipment
  • No food or drink without permission

On the Way In

  • Collect any papers on your way in
  • Only enter through the front door
  • Do the warmup if it is posted!
  • Backpack goes under the desk - don't block the aisle

During Class

  • When I chime the bell and raise my hand, stop what you are doing and listen
  • Your phone is away for the day

Working Time

  • Ask others for help before me
  • Stay on task and use your time wisely - do not disrupt others
  • Music and wired headphones are only allowed when doing independent work and only if you are on-task. Put them away when I am talking
  • No Chromebook games allowed. The only exception is chess, and only if you are done with all of your work. A better idea is to ask for an extra credit assignment
  • Stay in your seat unless:
    • You are getting supplies
    • You are getting tissues or hand sanitizer
    • You have gotten permission for any other reason
  • Only use AI to help you learn - do not copy and paste AI outputs and submit them as your own work. AI-generated work will receive a 0!

Using the Bathroom or Taking Breaks

  • One person is allowed in the bathroom at a time
  • Give me your cell phone before you go to the bathroom
  • Ask me if you need to take an unscheduled break

Respecting the Space

  • Stay away from my desk and cabinets
  • Stay out of the storage room/calming corner unless given permission
  • Treat any and all equipment with care - breaking anything will result in parent contact
  • No more than one person is allowed in the calming corner at a time, no exceptions

End of Class

  • When I tell you to start cleaning up, stop what you are doing, put materials away, and clean up your space
  • I will not dismiss you until the class is clean and you are seated and quiet
  • When dismissed, push your chair in
